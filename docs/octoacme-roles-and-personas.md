# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define quality standards and test coverage requirements
- Lead manual and automated testing activities
- Validate acceptance criteria compliance
- Report quality metrics and test coverage
- Collaborate with developers on testability and edge cases
- Participate in release readiness assessments

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet defined acceptance criteria
- Maximize test coverage and reduce post-release defects
- Enable fast, confident releases with minimal regression risk
- Make quality metrics visible and actionable

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Test case reviews and acceptance criteria discussions
- Daily standup updates on test progress and blockers
- Pre-release quality gates and smoke test coordination
- Incident retrospectives and root cause analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on test design and edge case identification
- Validates acceptance criteria with Product Managers
- Coordinates release readiness with Project Managers
- Reports quality status to Project Manager for risk escalation

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and resources. They represent customer needs and organizational strategy, remove organizational blockers, and make high-level decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project scope and budget
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Make priority and trade-off decisions with Product Manager
- Remove organizational blockers and resource constraints
- Approve releases and go/no-go decisions
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Attend milestone reviews and key decision gates

### Goals
- Deliver business value on schedule
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communications
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable fast decision-making and unblock teams

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and initiation reviews
- Milestone and release approvals
- Executive status updates (monthly or as needed)
- Escalation resolution and decision-making
- Post-release retrospectives and learnings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Manager on strategic priorities and success metrics
- Approves budgets and resources with Project Manager
- Reviews progress and risks at key gates
- Escalates blockers and removes organizational impediments

---

## Technical Architect/Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects/Technical Leads define technical strategy, architecture, and design patterns. They ensure scalability, security, and maintainability of solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Create technical design and architecture documentation
- Define coding standards and best practices
- Review technical designs and code for alignment with architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers on technical design and patterns
- Guide technology choices and trade-off decisions
- Ensure scalability, security, and performance requirements are met

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team velocity through clear technical direction
- Build systems that can scale and evolve

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews for complex or critical components
- Sprint planning for technical feasibility
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Knowledge sharing and team mentoring

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Manager on feasibility and trade-offs
- Guides Developers on technical design and implementation patterns
- Identifies technical risks for Project Manager escalation
- Collaborates with QA on non-functional requirements (performance, security)

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process impediments, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They ensure the team follows agreed working agreements and practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and process health
- Facilitate retrospectives and drive action items
- Remove organizational process blockers
- Help the team optimize cycle time and reduce waste

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and consistency
- Build psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Minimize process friction and blockers
- Enable faster decision-making and execution

### Typical Communication
- All agile ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Metrics review and process optimization discussions
- Executive updates on team health and process improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Facilitates all interactions and decision-making for the Project Manager
- Coaches Developers on process adherence and continuous improvement
- Works with QA on test automation and quality processes
- Supports Product Manager with backlog refinement ceremonies

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations/DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and production reliability. They ensure smooth deployments and rapid incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Design and manage deployment infrastructure and pipelines
- Automate testing, building, and deployment processes
- Monitor production systems and set up alerting
- Manage production incidents and coordinate rollbacks
- Document runbooks and operational procedures
- Collaborate on non-functional requirements (availability, performance)
- Support release coordination and validation
- Maintain system observability and logging

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments to production
- Maximize system uptime and reliability
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents
- Provide visibility into system health and performance

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- Non-functional requirements review (performance, security, scalability)
- Monitoring and alerting strategy discussions
- Runbook reviews with developers and QA

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on deployment testing and runbooks
- Coordinates release timing and readiness with Project Manager
- Validates release criteria with QA and Product Manager
- Provides production metrics and reliability status for stakeholder updates
